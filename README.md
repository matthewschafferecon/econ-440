# ECON 440: Money, Credit, and Monetary Policy

Course-development repository for Eastern Michigan University's Fall 2026
ECON 440 seminar and College Fed Challenge policy lab.

## Public course website

The deployable static site is in [`docs/`](docs/). It includes:

- the Fall 2026 schedule and October 5 production calendar;
- the College Fed Challenge rubric, research-desk system, and workflow;
- a student monetary-policy study guide and Q&A drill bank;
- an authoritative macroeconomic data and policy-source guide;
- the Fall 2026 advanced monetary-economics seminar sequence and assessments; and
- participation, privacy, student-authorship, and AI policies.

The site uses relative links and has no build dependencies. A GitHub Actions
workflow publishes the contents of `docs/` to GitHub Pages whenever `main`
changes.

## Course-development materials

Faculty-facing plans and reusable student templates are indexed in
[`course-materials/README.md`](course-materials/README.md).

## Privacy

Student names, competition drafts, forecasts, slides, feedback, rehearsal
recordings, and submission archives do not belong in this public repository.
Retained 2024-25 submissions and local source documents are excluded from
version control.
