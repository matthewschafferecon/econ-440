## Team structure

- [ ] Confirm that all potential team members and alternates are registered EMU
      students for fall 2026.

- [ ] Assign an initial owner and a backup for labor, inflation, activity,

## Official competition administration

- [ ] Verify EMU's 2026 Fed Challenge region from the official region tool.
- [ ] Confirm that EMU will submit only one team.
- [ ] Select the primary contact.
- [ ] Determine whether the team could attend the November 20 in-person final in
      Washington, D.C.
- [ ] Register faculty and students for the September 21 virtual orientation.
- [ ] Prepare names and email addresses for the adviser, team members, and
      alternates.
- [ ] Complete team registration before September 30 at 5:00 p.m. EDT.
- [ ] Save the confirmation and assigned team ID in the private course space.

## Production controls

- [ ] Freeze a common data vintage for each formal forecast round.
- [ ] Require metadata for every chart: source, series, units, transformation,
      frequency, date range, and retrieval date.
- [ ] Require students to distinguish data, forecast, assumption, and policy
      judgment.
- [ ] Run a complete claim-evidence audit before slides are frozen.
- [ ] Check that the four required content areas are explicit: current
      conditions, forecast, risks, and recommendation.
- [ ] Score at least two full rehearsals using the national five-category
      rubric.
- [ ] Give each of the five presenters a substantial and integral role.
- [ ] Verify that every presenter can answer all four official question types.
- [ ] Confirm that every word, chart choice, forecast, recommendation, slide,
      script, and practiced answer is original student work.

## Recording day

- [ ] Use no school name, student name, adviser name, Fed district, school
      color, logo, emblem, branded clothing, or identifiable background.
- [ ] Record a simple presentation; do not spend analytical time on elaborate
      production.
- [ ] Keep the final duration safely below 15:00.
- [ ] Check audio intelligibility, slide legibility, transitions, and presenter
      framing.
- [ ] Record early enough to permit a controlled technical re-recording.
- [ ] Preserve the exact slides, script outline, source log, and data vintage
      associated with the final video.

## Submission day: October 5

- [ ] Run the final anonymity, duration, authorship, and source checks.
- [ ] Upload the video to YouTube as **unlisted**.
- [ ] Use the exact title `College Fed Challenge 2026 Team # [assigned ID]`.
- [ ] Test the link while signed out or in a private window.
- [ ] Enter the unlisted link in the official submission form.
- [ ] Submit well before 5:00 p.m. EDT.
- [ ] Save the submission receipt, final link, exact video, and final supporting
      files privately.
- [ ] Have a second person verify receipt and archive completeness.

## After submission

- [ ] Conduct a team retrospective without making competition results part of
      course grading.
- [ ] Begin the advanced seminar.
- [ ] Maintain a short weekly Q&A drill until the October 27–30 notification
      window closes.
- [ ] Preserve the submitted presentation and data unchanged for any regional
      virtual Q&A.

## If selected for the November 2–4 virtual Q&A

- [ ] Confirm the assigned platform, time zone, login, camera, microphone, room,
      and backup device.
- [ ] Do not add new slides or data to the submitted presentation.
- [ ] Practice follow-up, hypothetical, theory, and Federal Reserve
      implementation questions.
- [ ] Run 15-minute simulations with two common questions and structured
      follow-ups.
- [ ] Decide how students will hand off, supplement, and correct answers without
      speaking over one another.

## If selected for the November 20 national final

- [ ] Confirm travel authority, funding, student availability, and required
      university paperwork immediately.
- [ ] Recheck the official finalist instructions.
- [ ] Update the original presentation with new data only as permitted.
- [ ] Preserve student authorship throughout every revision.
- [ ] Re-run the claim, source, chart, anonymity, duration, and rehearsal audits.
- [ ] Print the required presentation copies and prepare redundant digital
      copies.
