## Verification prompts

- Is the claim about a level, rate of change, acceleration, gap, or cause?
- Does the evidence measure that exact concept?
- Is the comparison period economically meaningful?
- Could revisions or seasonal adjustment change the conclusion?
- Is a group, sector, or region being generalized to the whole economy?
- Does the wording imply causation when the evidence shows only correlation?
- Would a reasonable analyst using the same evidence reach a different
  conclusion?
- What observation would falsify the claim?
