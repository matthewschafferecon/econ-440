## Internal-consistency checks

- [ ] Growth, employment, and inflation paths tell a coherent story.
- [ ] Financial conditions are consistent with the policy assumption.
- [ ] Consumption and income assumptions are compatible.
- [ ] Labor-supply assumptions are explicit.
- [ ] Forecast ranges are wider where evidence is weaker.
- [ ] Baseline forecast is distinguishable from current conditions.
- [ ] Differences from major benchmarks are explained rather than hidden.
