## Scenario discipline

For each high-priority risk, answer:

1. What is the initial shock?
2. Through which channels does it affect demand, supply, employment, inflation,
   or financial stability?
3. Which variables move first?
4. What magnitude or persistence makes the risk policy-relevant?
5. What would the team observe if the mechanism were wrong?
6. Would the preferred policy respond immediately, wait for confirmation, or
   use a different instrument?
7. How does the response change under a combined shock?
