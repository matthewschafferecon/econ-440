October 5

5:00 p.m. ET

Fall 2026 submission deadline

The first phase of the course focuses on building EMU's submission to the 2026 College Fed Challenge. Students will develop research, presentation, and policy-analysis skills as they prepare the first-round submission, due October 5. After the submission, the course shifts to an advanced seminar in monetary economics. Each week, the seminar examines an important topic in modern monetary economics and provides opportunities to deepen and apply the skills developed during the Fed Challenge portion of the course.

Part I

Fed Challenge Preparation

The scheduled assignments move from individual research and speaking to a complete team policy presentation, production, and reflection.

01 Sept. 1–10 Diagnose, research, and communicate
02 Sept. 15–17 Build and present a current-conditions briefing
03 Sept. 22–24 Formulate and defend a policy recommendation
04 Sept. 29–Oct. 8 Rehearse, produce, submit, and reflect
