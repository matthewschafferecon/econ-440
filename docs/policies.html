Course conduct

Attendance, deadlines, and teamwork

The Fed Challenge phase is collaborative and time-sensitive, so regular attendance, preparation, and timely communication are essential.

  • Notify the instructor promptly about an absence or circumstances that threaten a team deadline.
  • Meet assigned responsibilities, share work on time, and maintain usable source records.
  • Treat teammates, external judges, and guests professionally.
  • Expect team assignments to include individual work, contribution records, peer feedback, or instructor observation.
  • Alternative arrangements are considered when required by an accommodation, university policy, or documented emergency.

Original work

Student authorship and AI

The College Fed Challenge requires original student work. Current Federal Reserve rules and direct organizer instructions control if they are stricter.

Permitted tools

Non-generative research support

  • Calculators and spreadsheet formulas
  • Student-written and understood code
  • Databases, search tools, and citation managers
  • Spelling and accessibility checkers that do not generate or substantively rewrite content

Not permitted for competition work

Generative or substantive assistance

  • Economic analysis, forecasts, scenarios, or risks
  • Policy recommendations and supporting arguments
  • Slide language, structure, narration, scripts, or chart selection
  • Generated, rewritten, polished, critiqued, or simulated Q&A responses
Do not paste unpublished team work into an AI service.

A limited AI use may be authorized for a second-half seminar activity only when the instructor explicitly identifies the allowed tool, stage, disclosure, and evidence of the student’s own reasoning.

Roles

Enrollment and presenter selection

Registered students complete both phases. Other approved participants may join the Fed Challenge phase under instructor arrangements, but participation does not confer course credit.

Presenter selection
A competition-role decision based on the needs of the submission; it is separate from grading.
Non-presenter contributions
Research checks, visual aids, source verification, timing, recording, quality control, and other assigned production work can earn full credit.
Private workspace
Student names, drafts, forecasts, slides, feedback, recordings, deliberations, and submission archives remain outside this public site.
