Before class

What to extract from a reading

Analyze the argument; do not merely summarize it.

Question
What uncertainty or disagreement is the reading designed to resolve?
Mechanism
What behavior, friction, or institution generates the result?
Evidence
What data or model discipline the claim?
Assumption
Which assumption is doing the most work?
Challenge
What is the strongest objection or alternative interpretation?
Policy
What would a policymaker do differently if the result is correct?
