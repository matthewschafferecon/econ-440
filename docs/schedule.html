Fall 2026

Course schedule

Tuesdays and Thursdays, 12:30–1:45 p.m. The opening sequence uses ten dated production meetings before the October 5 submission deadline.

Built-in contingency.

After October 5, the seminar begins immediately. A short weekly Q&A block remains available until regional selections are announced October 27–30. Regional Q&As run November 2–4; the in-person final is November 20.

Part I

Fed Challenge policy lab

Sept. 1 & 3

Week 1 · Foundations and baseline

Establish rules, research desks, shared definitions, and the first economic dashboard.

Tue. Sept. 1 · Mandate, rules, and diagnostic

Competition requirements, rubric, FOMC structure, authorship, and skill inventory.

Thu. Sept. 3 · Implementation and transmission

Ample reserves, administered rates, balance-sheet tools, channels, and lags.

Sept. 8 & 10

Week 2 · Current conditions

Move from raw releases to defensible claims about inflation, employment, demand, and credit.

Tue. Sept. 8 · Inflation and price stability

CPI versus PCE, recent momentum, composition, persistence, expectations, and supply.

Thu. Sept. 10 · Labor, activity, and inclusion

Payrolls, unemployment, participation, GDP/GDI, demand, revisions, and disparities.

Sept. 15 & 17

Week 3 · Forecast and risks

Create one internally consistent baseline and identify the conditions that would overturn it.

Tue. Sept. 15 · Credit and financial conditions

Yield curves, spreads, banks, household credit, lending standards, and transmission.

Thu. Sept. 17 · FOMC synthesis and forecast

Horizon, assumptions, benchmarks, ranges, reconciliation, and forecast ownership.

Sept. 22 & 24

Week 4 · Policy decision

Use the September FOMC meeting and new SEP as evidence, not as an answer key.

Tue. Sept. 22 · Risks and decision framework

Alternative scenarios, signposts, policy tradeoffs, distributional effects, and options.

Thu. Sept. 24 · Presentation architecture

Statement, implementation note, SEP, press conference, and student-owned presentation logic.

Sept. 29 & Oct. 1

Week 5 · Rehearse, record, submit

Refresh final releases, freeze the argument, stress-test the team, and control the submission.

Tue. Sept. 29 · Full rehearsal and adversarial review

Rubric scoring, four Q&A categories, chart verification, and prioritized revision.

Thu. Oct. 1 · Recording and submission control

Timing, anonymity, technical review, unlisted upload, playback check, and confirmation archive.
