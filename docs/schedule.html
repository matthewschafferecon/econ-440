Course schedule

Tuesdays and Thursdays, 12:30–1:45 p.m. The Fed Challenge phase runs through October 8; the advanced monetary-economics seminar begins October 13.

Advancement contingency.

If EMU advances, November 3 may be used for regional Q&A preparation and November 19 may be used for national-final preparation. Affected seminar material will be compressed, reassigned, or moved; the 50/50 grading split will not change.
